### CHEM-000565: Manufacturing-tolerant high-nickel cathode chemomechanics
- **Subfield:** Electrochemical energy-storage chemistry
- **Open:** Prevent oxygen loss, pore evolution and mechanical damage while retaining energy density across realistic loading, voltage, temperature and manufacturing variation.
- **Status:** A longstanding tradeoff. A 2026 uniform-pore architecture showed negligible degradation under studied conditions; robustness to industrial heterogeneity remains open.
- **Type:** engineering
- **Why:** It would retain high capacity without continually increasing inactive protective material.
